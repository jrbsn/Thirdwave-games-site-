Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers Pizza Mogul: Restaurant Tycoon and any other games published by Thirdwave Games LLC (“Thirdwave Games”, “we”).

We do not collect any personal data. Pizza Mogul does not ask you for an account, does not contain advertising or analytics, and does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. The game does not need an internet connection.

Purchases. The full-game unlock is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy terms; we receive no payment details and nothing that identifies you.

Your saved games stay on your device. The game stores your saves in the app’s own storage on your phone or tablet. If you use your device’s own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup), your saves may be included in that backup under your device maker’s terms. If you choose to export a save file, you decide where it goes.

Children. The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds an online feature, we will update this policy and say so in the app’s release notes before the change takes effect.

Contact. Questions about this policy: info@thirdwave.games
