Pizza Mogul: Restaurant Tycoon

Coming soon to Google Play

Pizza Mogul is a turn-based business game about running a pizza chain. You start with one shop, a bank loan and plenty of competition.

Sites

Each city has a map of sites to rent. Slice counters do well near stations, sit-down pizzerias need foot traffic, and delivery kitchens can sit on cheaper back streets. Each store delivers within its own area, so two stores close together split the orders.

Stores

You set the menu and prices, buy kitchen equipment, and hire cooks, drivers and managers. Each store has its own sales, costs and reviews. Equipment breaks, staff quit, and rival chains cut their prices when you open near them.

Head office

Once you have a few stores you can hire a head office: operations, marketing, people, finance, technology and more. Your chiefs handle the day-to-day and bring the bigger decisions to you.

Growing

There are 32 cities in 12 countries, including New York, London, Naples, Tokyo and São Paulo. Your first city is free; the full game opens the rest. Later on you can franchise, sell pizza in supermarkets, take out loans, bring in investors and buy rival chains.

Price

Free to play your first city, with no time limit and no store cap. A one-time $4.99 purchase unlocks the world: every other city and country, franchising, buying out rival chains, your own supply chain and pizza in supermarkets. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.
